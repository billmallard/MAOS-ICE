# Engine failure-mode ledger, v0 (seed)

Owner: PROPULSION, holding it for SAFETY, which is paused (ORG-009). Issues: AER-3320 (seed), AER-3326 (phase 1: FL-24 to FL-29), AER-3329 (phase 1b: FL-30 to FL-37), AER-3331 (castings: FL-38, FL-39; FL-34 reworded), AER-3411 (crank routes, gearbox, supercharger: FL-40 to FL-49, each conditional on the route being taken), AER-3439 (crank H-301: FL-50 to FL-53).

This is a seed, not a hazard analysis. It records every single failure that phase 0 raised and what each leaves the pilot, so SAFETY inherits a list when it resumes. Nothing here has been tested. "Leaves" is a design intent or a physics expectation, not a demonstrated result.

| FL-31 | Turbocharger bearing or oil seal | seal or bearing failure | oil into the intake (smoke, possible knock) or the exhaust (smoke, fire risk); then unboosted power. | FL-37 | Wastegate actuator stuck (MFG-5) | build or wear: actuator rod or gate seized | as FL-30. Mitigation on the build side: a manifold-pressure check at run-up | envelope 10.4 |
| FL-38 | Crankcase half with the cylinder bank cast in (concept H, ruling 1) | porosity path or crack between the coolant jacket and the crank bay or a drilled oil passage | coolant in the oil, then bearing damage over minutes to hours; coolant loss as FL-27. | FL-39 | Head jacket plug (the unused coolant boss on each head, concept H) | plug backs out or cracks | loss of coolant: about 1 minute at MCP (FL-27). Mitigation: a threaded plug with sealant, staked or lockwired; never a Welch or cup plug on a coolant jacket (DR-7) | CASTINGS_H.md ruling 7 |
| FL-40 | Reduction gearbox, proven-class design (refines FL-1; only if a geared variant is taken) | gear tooth, bearing, shaft or spline failure | total loss of thrust.
