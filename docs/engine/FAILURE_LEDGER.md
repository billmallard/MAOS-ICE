# Engine failure-mode ledger, v0 (seed)

Owner: PROPULSION, holding it for SAFETY, which is paused (ORG-009). Issues: AER-3320 (seed), AER-3326 (phase 1: FL-24 to FL-29), AER-3329 (phase 1b: FL-30 to FL-37), AER-3331 (castings: FL-38, FL-39; FL-34 reworded), AER-3411 (crank routes, gearbox, supercharger: FL-40 to FL-49, each conditional on the route being taken), AER-3439 (crank H-301: FL-50), AER-3525 (a Hayabusa core as a first flying engine: FL-51 to FL-60, each conditional on that route being taken), AER-3631 (the program FADEC flown on a bought Aeromomentum AM15: FL-61 to FL-69, each conditional on the mule route).

This is a seed, not a hazard analysis. It records every single failure that phase 0 raised and what each leaves the pilot, so SAFETY inherits a list when it resumes. Nothing here has been tested. "Leaves" is a design intent or a physics expectation, not a demonstrated result.

| FL-33 | First-op datum across the case pair (MFG-1) | lifter-to-cam or main misalignment | bearing or lifter distress. Mitigation: one setup on the doweled pair, a CMM report with the first article, Plastigage at assembly | envelope 10.4 |

The merged case jacket is open to the deck, so it is cleaned through the deck (CASTINGS_H.md ruling 7) | envelope 9.5 item 7, 10.4; CASTINGS_H.md |
| FL-35 | Darton MID liner O-ring (MFG-3) | O-ring pinched at install | coolant in the oil, then bearing damage. Mitigation: a lead-in chamfer spec and a jacket pressure test after liner install (B1). The sleeve is fitted uncut, so a shop cut never removes Darton's chamfer or reaches the O-ring land, and its O-ring end seals in a cast lower barrel (AER-3442) | envelope 10.4; CASTINGS_H 9.4 |
| FL-36 | Drilled-gallery plug (MFG-4) | plug backs out | oil loss: minutes to bearing failure. Mitigation: threaded plugs, sealant and staking; no cup plugs on pressurized galleries | envelope 9.5 item 6, 10.4 |
| FL-37 | Wastegate actuator stuck (MFG-5) | build or wear: actuator rod or gate seized | as FL-30. Mitigation on the build side: a manifold-pressure check at run-up | envelope 10.4 |

Mitigation: at least 5 mm of metal from any oil passage to a jacket (DR-6), radiography of every casting before first-op and a jacket pressure test before the liners go in (DR-8), and an oil-condition check at each oil change | CASTINGS_H.md section 2 |
| FL-39 | Head jacket plug (the unused coolant boss on each head, concept H) | plug backs out or cracks | loss of coolant: about 1 minute at MCP (FL-27). Mitigation: a threaded plug with sealant, staked or lockwired; never a Welch or cup plug on a coolant jacket (DR-7) | CASTINGS_H.md ruling 7 |
| FL-40 | Reduction gearbox, proven-class design (refines FL-1; only if a geared variant is taken) | gear tooth, bearing, shaft or spline failure | total loss of thrust. | FL-41 | Gearbox overload (slip) clutch | slips under normal torque (set low or worn), or seizes | slipping: propeller below engine rpm, lost thrust and heat in the clutch; seized: a propeller strike reaches the crank, as direct drive. | FL-42 | Propeller-shaft decoupler (route 2, direct drive) | disc-pack or gear coupling failure, or propeller-shaft bearing failure | coupling: total loss of thrust; bearing: rumble, then propeller-shaft wobble and seizure. | FL-44 | Built-up crank, press-fit or bolted joints (route 1) | a joint slips under a torque reversal, backfire or propeller strike | the throw phase moves: crank-sensor timing error on both lanes, then rod or counterweight clash; total loss. | FL-45 | Roller big ends (route 1a) | needle-cage fatigue | L10 about 700 h at cruise for a 105 mm bore on a 50 kN cage: inside the TBO. Spalling, then rod failure | CRANK_AND_SCALING.md section 3.1 |
| FL-46 | Cast iron crank (route 3) | shrinkage or a non-nodular zone at a fillet | crack, then crank failure: total loss. Mitigation: radiography and magnetic-particle inspection of every crank, a propeller decoupler so the nose carries no bending | CRANK_AND_SCALING.md section 3.3 |
| FL-47 | 180-degree V6 balance shaft (route 4) | balance-shaft drive fails | a free primary couple of about 1,000-2,100 N m at 55 Hz into the engine mount | CRANK_AND_SCALING.md section 3.4 |
| FL-48 | Supercharger bypass valve (only if the supercharger is chosen) | valve stuck closed | with the ratio set at the climb rpm, the compressor can make a pressure ratio of about 1.8 at sea level and 3,300 rpm: a manifold near 1.8 bar, heavy knock. Mitigation: a mechanical relief valve, a FADEC overboost cut of spark and fuel, and a run-up manifold-pressure check | CRANK_AND_SCALING.md section 6 |
| FL-49 | Supercharger belt drive | belt failure | unboosted power (65 % at 12,000 ft), belt debris at the crank nose. Mitigation: belt guard, a belt-condition item on the inspection list | RECOST_PHASE1B.md section 6; CRANK_AND_SCALING.md section 6 |

| FL-51 | Hayabusa conversion: soft coupling in the reduction drive (AER-3525) | the elastomer or spring coupling ages, hardens or is replaced with a stiffer part | the drive's first mode rises toward the idle 2nd order: at 0.7 x the idle firing frequency instead of 0.5 x, the alternating torque at the gear mesh goes from about 1.1 to about 2.9 x the mean at idle (gear hammer, then FL-2 and FL-1). | FL-52 | Hayabusa conversion with one plug per cylinder (stock head) | one coil, plug, plug lead or injector driver fails | one cylinder dead: about 72 % of MCP, below R-25's 90 %. It also puts half- and first-order torque into a drive tuned below idle: the half order crosses its mode near 3,400 crank rpm, in the descent band, with the mesh torque reversing at about 3 x the mean. Mitigation: a second plug per cylinder (head modification, AER-3526), or a published restricted-rpm band after a cylinder loss, with the FADEC annunciating which cylinder is dead | HAYABUSA_PLATFORM.md section 5; `torsional_busa.py` section 4 |
| FL-53 | Hayabusa conversion driving through the motorcycle's own clutch and gearbox | the wet clutch slips at takeoff torque | engine rpm rises, thrust falls, at the worst moment. This is the builders' conclusion on the one Hayabusa aircraft found, the Bugatti 100P replica (NTSB CEN16FA307, fatal, 2016; the NTSB named an engine anomaly of undetermined cause). Design rule: no motorcycle clutch in the torque path; the only slipping element is a calibrated overload clutch at about 2-2.7 x rated torque (FL-41) | HAYABUSA_PLATFORM.md section 4; NTSB CEN16FA307 |
| FL-54 | Hayabusa cam chain and tensioner | tensioner fails or the chain stretches; the chain jumps a tooth | valve-to-piston contact: engine failure, total loss of thrust. Gen 1 tensioner recalls 99V270000 / 00V319000; race-car users report Gen 2 (K8) tensioner chain failures and refit the earlier type (forum). Mitigation: the proven tensioner, chain and guides replaced at a fixed interval (Radical replaces them at every rebuild), and a FADEC cam-to-crank phase check that flags stretch before it jumps | HAYABUSA_PLATFORM.md section 3 |
| FL-55 | Hayabusa wet sump in aircraft attitudes | oil surge at climb pitch, in sideslip or under negative g in turbulence; or oil above about 120 C | bearing failure: minutes. Car racers report the wet sump cannot be controlled, and SBD reports idle oil pressure as low as 2 psi above 120 C. Mitigation: a dry sump (SBD kit in production), an oil cooler holding 80-100 C, oil pressure and temperature on the panel with an alarm | HAYABUSA_PLATFORM.md section 3 |
| FL-56 | Hayabusa charging system (one stator, one regulator) | stator or regulator fails (Gen 2 regulator recall 11V108) | both FADEC lanes, the fuel pumps and the coils run on the battery; endurance is the battery's minutes. | FL-57 | Hayabusa at the stock 12.5:1 compression on 91 AKI (MON 85) at sustained full throttle | knock below about 9,000 rpm | piston, ring-land or gasket damage, then power loss. knock.py puts the limit at about 11.2-11.7 between 6,000 and 7,000 rpm, even with the liquid-cooled-head credit. | FL-58 | Gen 3 Hayabusa ride-by-wire throttle (only if a Gen 3 core is used) | the throttle actuator or its FADEC channel fails | the throttle returns to its spring limp-home position, near idle: a single failure takes most of the power away (assumed from the usual electronic-throttle design; the Gen 3 limp position was not sourced). | FL-59 | Hayabusa bottom end in aircraft duty (no aviation hours anywhere) | rod or main bearing distress under hours of high load | bearing failure, then total loss. The only high-load evidence is Radical's quoted rebuild intervals (90-100 h for the 1340 at race duty, all bearings replaced). Mitigation: R-41 teardowns at 25, 100 and 300 h; oil analysis at every change; a provisional interval set from the teardowns, not assumed | HAYABUSA_PLATFORM.md section 3 |
| FL-60 | Second spark plug machined into a Hayabusa head (only if the head modification is taken; MANUFACTURING, AER-3526) | the bore weakens the ligament to a pressed-in valve-seat insert, or the sleeved plug tube leaks | a seat loosens under thermal cycling and drops: engine destroyed, total loss of thrust. A sleeve leaking into the chamber: coolant loss and, at worst, hydraulic lock; into the cam box: coolant in the oil. Mitigation: a minimum ligament to each seat from a sectioned scrap head before any hole is drawn, a prove-out head machined, pressure-tested and sectioned, a jacket pressure test after machining, a leak-down check at each condition inspection, the plug tip flush with the roof to avoid a hot crevice | HAYABUSA_PLATFORM.md section 5; AER-3526 |
| FL-61 | Program FADEC in place of a conversion vendor's ECU (AER-3631) | the new calibration runs lean or over-advanced at high power | detonation, then a lifted head gasket, a holed piston or a seizure: power loss to total loss. The field case: a Zenith CH 750 lost its Viking 130 after a replacement ECU ran lean (GA News 2019, NTSB number not found). Mitigation: map the vendor's calibration on the stand first (G1) and match it within stated limits per lane (G2); a wideband sensor and per-cylinder EGT on each lane; knock control (R-13); flight only on a tagged, stand-proven calibration | FADEC_MULE.md sections 1, 3.4 |
| FL-62 | Lane arbitration (AER-3631) | the element that hands the outputs from one lane to the other fails: it cuts both lanes, toggles, or holds a dead lane | total loss of power, or a rough engine with no fault annunciated. The field case: NTSB ERA23FA023 (Viking 110, 2 fatal): a dual-ECU select switch and its wiring cut the engine. Mitigation: no pilot-operated select switch in the power path; a hardware watchdog per lane that fails to the last good lane; the arbitration board powered from both feeds; every failure state of the board fault-injected on the bench (G0) and on a running engine (G3); a lane test at every run-up | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.1 |
| FL-63 | Two lanes wired to one injector or coil (AER-3631) | the idle lane's output driver fails shorted on | an injector held open (flooding, washed bore, hydraulic lock at worst) or a coil held charged (coil burnout, then that cylinder dead). The OR-ing defeats the healthy lane. Mitigation: a high-side switch per lane on each injector, so only the active lane is connected; current sensing and a fuse per output; the shorted-driver case in G0 and G3 | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.1 |
| FL-64 | Both lanes running the same firmware (AER-3631; applies to concept H as well) | one software fault, triggered by the same input in both lanes | both lanes misbehave together: the lane redundancy does not cover it. Mitigation: flight only on a tagged release that has passed G0-G5; a minimal fixed-map limp mode in each lane that is independent of the main fuel and spark code; input range and rate checks before any value reaches a control law; the vendor ECU kept as a ground spare, not as a switchable third lane (FL-62) | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.2 |
| FL-65 | AM15 mule with one plug and one coil per cylinder (AER-3631; as FL-52) | one coil, plug or plug lead fails; or one wasted-spark coil pack fails, if the vendor's packs are kept | one cylinder dead: 70-72 % of takeoff power, below R-25's 90 %. A wasted-spark pack fires two cylinders: 40-44 %. A dead cylinder also puts half and first orders into the Guibo-coupled gearbox, not yet surveyed. Mitigation: coil-on-plug, one coil per cylinder; the FADEC annunciates which cylinder is dead; a restricted-rpm advisory after any cylinder loss until the G3 survey; the mule is a declared reduced-redundancy exception to R-25 | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.3; `mule.py` section 2 |
| FL-66 | Shared trigger hardware on the mule (AER-3631) | the cam sensor fails; or the program trigger wheel, read by both lanes' Hall sensors, loosens or loses a tooth | cam: both lanes drop to crank-only operation (wasted spark, batch injection), power about unchanged, annunciated. Wheel: both lanes lose position together: total loss, unless the vendor's own crank sensor, kept as a third input, is used as the fallback. Mitigation: the wheel keyed and bolted, a witness mark at each inspection, a continuous cross-check of the program wheel against the vendor's sensor. On concept H the same applies to H-301's single reluctor (CRANK_H301 item 7) | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.2 |
| FL-67 | AM15 charging system: one alternator (AER-3631; as FL-56) | the alternator or regulator fails | lane A, the pumps and the coils run on the main battery, and lane B on its own battery: the endurance is the batteries' minutes. Mitigation: lane B's battery sized for at least 30 min of lane B, its coils, injectors and one pump; a charging annunciation; a load-shed list | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.3 |
| FL-68 | Aeromomentum gearbox under the program's calibration (AER-3631) | bearing-retainer screws back out on a vibrating installation (Aeromomentum SB 12/2/19: 3 cases); or a changed idle speed, spark scatter or a dead cylinder moves the drive onto a torsional resonance the vendor did not test | gearbox bearing distress, then total loss of thrust (FL-40, FL-2). Mitigation: screws safety-wired and checked before each flight per the SB; the vendor's idle speed kept; the propeller within the vendor's 6,000 kg cm^2 inertia limit; a torsional survey with all cylinders live and one dead (G3) | FADEC_MULE.md sections 2.5, 3.4 |
| FL-69 | Second injector per cylinder, split across the lanes (AER-3631, only if fitted) | one lane drops at takeoff power | each cylinder loses half its fuel until the surviving lane doubles its pulse width: a lean step of about one engine cycle, then normal running. Mitigation: takeover inside one cycle (21 ms at 5,800 rpm), measured in G0 and on the running engine in G3 with lambda and EGT logged | FADEC_MULE.md section 3.1 |
