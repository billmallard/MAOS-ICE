## E15 and pump octane

EPA defines E15 as 10.5-15 % ethanol, approved only for 2001-and-newer cars and light trucks and flex-fuel vehicles [E1]. Summer sale (June 1 - Sept 15) still runs on emergency waivers; 2026 was the fifth year (waivers 2026-03-25 and 2026-05-19) [E2][E3]. Year-round E15 is not law: the House passed H.R. 1346 in May 2026 (218-203); no Senate passage found [E3][E4]. ASTM D4814 does not cap oxygenate content in its scope and carries antiknock guidance (seasonal, altitude) in Appendix X1 [E5]. ASTM D2699 states the antiknock index (RON+MON)/2 "in accordance with Specification D4814" is what is "posted on retail dispensing pumps in the U.S." [E6]. The 87/89/91/93 grade ladder was not verified from a primary source (the D4814 appendix is paywalled).
