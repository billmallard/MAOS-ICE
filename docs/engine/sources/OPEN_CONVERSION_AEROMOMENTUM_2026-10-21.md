## 1. Aeromomentum (Stuart FL): current line

The site menu lists AM13/105hp, AM15/120hp, AM15T/160HP, AM13T/140hp and AM20T/230hp-300hp [A1]. No 6-cylinder model was found. The home page says "Prices start at just $8,495 fully assembled From 75hp to 260hp!" [A1]. No model page shows $8,495 or 75 hp, so that claim is unexplained. It may be the 1000cc 3-cyl that Kitplanes mentions [K1][K2]. That model has no page on the site.

No page found gives a separate continuous rating. The maintenance card and manual say "RPM: 1800 to 5800 rpm normal operating with 6500 rpm redline" and "for longer engine life and better economy cruise power should be 65% or less" [A10][A9].

**Inclusions (AM13 / AM15):** "Our pricing includes engine, gearbox, starter, alternator, ECU, wiring, mounting ears (to fit Lycoming style mount)" [A2][A3]. **The radiator and exhaust are not included.** They are sold separately [A13]:
- radiator $205–345
- exhaust header $735
- muffler $139
- fuel pump kit $585
- oil cooler $310

The manual says "installation adds about 25% to the base engine weight" [A8].

**Base engine:** "The AM family of engines are based on the well proven Suzuki G series automotive, light truck, marine and outboard base engines… aluminum block and head with single overhead cam and 16 valves" [A9].
- Kitplanes: "Suzuki is the source of the 1300 and 1500 engines, while the others are non-Suzuki" [K2].
- Kitplanes also says they "purchase industrial or long-block core engines from big automakers" [K2].
- Inference, not a source statement: 1298cc with 74×75.5 bore and stroke matches the Suzuki G13B/G13BB [W1]. 75×84.5 matches the Suzuki G15A (Wikipedia gives 1493cc) [W1]. Aeromomentum does not name the exact code.

**New vs used:** "We buy all brand new parts from OEM suppliers and assemble them into the complete engine in Stuart FL USA. We test run each engine with a propeller before shipment" [A7]. Also: "We never use any unknown JDM, used, 'rebuilt' or other junk yard parts" [A2].

**ECU / ignition:**
- Model pages say "FADEC system", "Semi Sequential Injection" [A2–A5].
- Manual: "The AM family of engines use the MicroSquirt ECU" [A9]. That is a single ECU. Its only redundancy is electrical supply: "the engine will continue to run on either the battery or the alternator alone" [A9].
- Fuel and ignition are "controlled by an open source port fuel injection system" [A9].
- Parts list [A12]:
  - "ECU EC1-009 – $450"
  - "Coil Pack (Set of 2, three-pin) EC1-005-1 – $89.30". Two packs suggest wasted-spark coil packs, not coil-on-plug (inference).
  - "Fuel Injector (4 required) – $62 each". That is one injector per cylinder, each with 4 orifices (manual: "(16) tiny orifices") [A9].
- The maintenance card lists one plug type, NGK BKR6EIX-11, at 0.042" gap [A10]. No twin-plug configuration was found, so the engine has one plug per cylinder.
- **No backup or dual ECU option was found** on any parts page [A12][A13]. A 2017 forum comment questioned the "Single ignition system" [F3].
- **Conflict:** Kitplanes 2025 says "Bosch engine management, sequential fuel injection and 65-amp alternators" [K2]. The manual says MicroSquirt and the model pages say semi-sequential.

**Fuel:** "Standard power versions of the AM13 and AM15 require 90 octane (AKI) or better gasoline. High power versions require 93 octane (AKI)… 100LL is 99.5 MON minimum or about 95 AKI. While the engine items we provide are compatible with up to 10% ethanol there may be incompatibility with your fuel tanks… If using 100LL we recommend… ALCOR… TCP" [A9]. The maintenance card adds: "If using 100LL fuel use semi-synthetic oil". Oil and gear-oil changes are every 50 h [A10].

**Gearbox:** Kitplanes describes it as a helical spur-gear PSRU with "custom 8620 alloy gears, designed in-house" [K2]. It is sold standalone [A12]:
- "Gear Box Complete GB1-A01 – $2,495"
- "Housing Only – $1,295"
- "Gear Set – $995"

The manual sets a prop limit: "Maximum Permitted Propeller Flange Moment of Inertia is 6000 kgcm2 (2050 lb.in2)". The hub is drilled for the Rotax 75mm, Rotax 4" and SAE-1 patterns [A9].

**Engine parts sold standalone** [A12]:
- Long block: AM13 $3,295; AM15 $4,295
- Short block: AM13 $2,225; AM15 $3,225
- Cylinder head: $995
- Bare block: AM13 $1,541; AM15 $2,191
- Crankshaft: AM13 $650; AM15 $760

**Warranty:** "two years from original ship date from AMI or 1 year from first run or 200 hours running time". It covers "original manufacturing and material defects".
- It is voided by "commercial use of any type… overheating, detonation, incorrect fuel… extended high power use… installation errors" and similar causes.
- The engine must be run within 90 days of shipment and then every 40 days, or be preserved for storage.
- The customer pays transport to and from AMI [A11].

**Manuals:** These are published publicly as PDFs [A8]:
- the Installation Manual V2
- the Internal Build Manual
- the Maintenance Card
- the gearbox filling instruction
- the Installation and Service Bulletins of 2 Dec 2019

**Fleet size / hours:** Not found. The "Planes with our Engines" page lists 7 aircraft types and makes no count or hours claim [A14]. A 2017 forum comment said "Only three flying so far" [F3].

- **Gearbox:** All models use "2.33/1". The site describes a "3 gear gearbox for Lycoming style propeller rotation" and a "4 coil ignition system" [V1]. Kitplanes says "climb or cruise gear ratios can be ordered" [K2].
- **Inclusions:** These are listed for the 130 and the 195T: "a complete running engine" with "40A alternator, throttle body, ECU, wire harness, and starter" [V7][V10]. Kitplanes 2024 priced the V-140T at "$16,500 with gearbox" [K1]. The radiator ($995) and engine mount ($1,500) are sold separately [V6].
- **Core:** Viking uses used cores: "We start with the most up to date year engines with super low mileage, derived from fender benders across the US" [V11]. Kitplanes: "buying used Japanese and domestic auto engines" [K1].
- **ECU redundancy:** No dual or backup ECU was found on the site [V1][V12]. The wiring page says a power short to ground will "burn up the ECU making it unrepairable". It puts the cost at $1,500 for the ECU plus about $815 of other parts [V12]. The warranty is void with "altered or different ECUs" [V13].
- **TBO:** Not found on any Viking page.
- **Warranty:** "12 months or 100 hours, whichever happens first. Warranty starts on the day of shipment". It is void if 100LL is used without a lead scavenger, with lithium/BMS batteries, or with non-Viking cowlings [V13].
- **Fuel:** 89–91+ octane car fuel. "100LL (MUST use TCP/Decalin)" [V2][V3][V8].

## 3. Field record / problems

- **AM15 fuel-rail bracket failure [FORUM]** [F1], Zenith forum, 1 Mar 2022: "Kevin Perreau… engine failure recently (AM-15)… The engine failure was caused by a broken bracket that secures the fuel rail."
  - Another owner reported "both of his brackets were cracked (but had not yet failed)".
  - Aeromomentum (Mark) blamed items hung off the rail and said "they are making stronger brackets".
- **Aeromomentum gearbox screws [MFR]** [A15], Service bulletin 2 Dec 2019: "We have had 3 instances of these screws [gearbox main bearing retainer] coming loose over time. In all cases they were on engines that had a noticeable vibration."
  - These screws are now shipped safety-wired.
  - The same bulletin covers starter-solenoid failures from undersized wiring and a cam-phase cover oil leak.
- **Aeromomentum NTSB record:** No NTSB report naming an Aeromomentum engine was found in web searches. CAROL was not queried directly. Treat this as "not found", not as "none exist".
- **Viking 110 gearbox drive-flange failures [FORUM, SNIPPET]** [F2]: VAF thread "Viking 110 notice". The page itself returned 403, so only the search snippet was seen: "In the last 6 months there have been 2 in-flight fatigue failures of the gearbox input drive flange ears… One failed at 130 hours total, the second… approximately 800 hours. Both failures occurred during take off, one caused the total loss of the airplane." The date was not seen.
- **Viking 110 torsion damper [FORUM]** [F4]: Zenith forum, about 1 Nov 2022. The gearbox "spider/plate" lost one of three segments, which exited the aircraft through the cowling and damaged the coolant pipe and radiator. The aircraft landed safely. Jan Eggenfellner (Viking) replied that the owner had ignored a service bulletin issued about 8 years earlier that offered free replacement parts.
- **Fleet size:** No FAA or NTSB fleet count for either brand was found.

## 4. Suzuki core production status

- **G13B (DOHC):** "1986–1994". **G13BB (SOHC 16V, 1298cc, 74×75.5):** introduced 1995 [W1].
  - The Maruti Gypsy (G13BB, "1.3 liter… 80 hp") ended civilian production in March 2019 [S1].
  - The Indian Army then ordered 3,051 Gypsys and production restarted, per Rushlane, 4 Jun 2019 [S2].
  - Current (2026) G13BB production: not found.
- **G15A:** 75×84.5, 1493cc, SOHC 16V [W1]. The end of production was not found.
  - The Suzuki DF70A/DF90A outboards use a 1502cc DOHC engine with 75×85 bore and stroke, per dealer pages [SNIPPET] [S3]. That is a related bore but not the same engine.
- **M13A** (78×69.5), **M15A** (78×78), **M16A** (78×83): the M family "replaced the long-lived G engine family". Wikipedia gives no end-of-production date. The latest application listed for the M15A is "2016–2022" (S-Cross Indonesia) [W2].
- **K15B:** 1462cc, 74×85, DOHC 16V VVT, 101–105 hp, "2018–present" (Jimny Sierra, Ertiga) [W3].
  - In India, the K15C replaced the K15B in most models. The Jimny keeps the K15B because Suzuki "wasn't willing to re-engineer [K15C]" for longitudinal mounting (Autocar India, 23 May 2023) [S4].
  - The Jimny was withdrawn from the EU in early 2025 but continues in Japan and India [SNIPPET] [S5].
- **J20A:** 1995cc, 84×90, DOHC; applications 2001–2012 (Grand Vitara 2005–2012) [W4].
- **What Aeromomentum says about core supply:** Nothing was found on its site. Kitplanes reports long-block or industrial cores bought from automakers, with Suzuki for the 1.3 and 1.5 [K2].
- **Core prices:** No new crate G13BB price was found; eBay returned 403. The only "new" long-block prices found are Aeromomentum's own: AM13 $3,295 and AM15 $4,295 [A12].
