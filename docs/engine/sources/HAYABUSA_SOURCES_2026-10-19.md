How much to trust them. Labels inside each note: primary (Suzuki documents, NTSB, NHTSA recalls, the Rotax installation manual), secondary (magazines, Wikipedia), forum, and snippet (the page blocked fetching and the text is a search engine's summary). Several numbers that carry weight in the study are forum or snippet only, and the study says so where it uses them: the engine mass (86-92 kg), Radical's rebuild intervals, the Bugatti team's clutch-slip conclusion and the Gen 2 oil-surge reports. Some Suzuki manual pages were read through a summarizing fetch and are worth spot-checking against a paper manual.
