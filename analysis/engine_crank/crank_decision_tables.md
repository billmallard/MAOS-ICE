## 7. Paired rotating and reciprocating mass for the balance note

The opposed pair in each bay runs on pins 180 deg apart, so each pair's rotating and reciprocating forces cancel as forces and leave a couple on the bank offset; the three bays' couples sum to zero (section 1). The crank is therefore balanced alone, with no bob weight, provided rods are matched end for end and pistons by weight across each opposed pair.
