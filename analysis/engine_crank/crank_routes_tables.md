## 5. Route 4: 180-degree V6 on 3 shared pins

Most even firing order available over 720 deg: TDCs at [0, 120, 180, 300, 420, 600]; intervals [120, 60, 120, 120, 180, 120] deg (even would be 120 throughout).
Free primary couple at 3,300 rpm: primary force per pin 10.9 kN (both rods), couple 2,114 N m at 55 Hz; crank counterweights can halve it to a 1,057 N m rotating couple; a counter-rotating balance shaft removes the rest.
H's flat-6: all primary and secondary forces and couples cancel (the three bank-offset couples sum to zero at 120 deg).
- H 6-throw (torsional_h.py): modes 261 / 768 / 1274 Hz; 6-cylinder major-order crossings (orders 3, 6, 9) idle to 105 %: mode 1 x order 6 at 2,610 rpm; mode 1 x order 9 at 1,740 rpm
- 180-degree V6, 3 throws: modes 358 / 1015 / 1559 Hz; 6-cylinder major-order crossings (orders 3, 6, 9) idle to 105 %: mode 1 x order 9 at 2,385 rpm

3-throw crank, specialist, first engine: $5.3k-7.6k (bar 168 kg, length 541 mm) against 6-throw $6.7k-9.3k; a balance shaft, gears and bearings add about $0.3k-1.0k and 3-5 kg [E].
