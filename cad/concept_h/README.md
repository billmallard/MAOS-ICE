# Concept H castings: coarse STEP envelopes (AER-3331; revised to crank H-301 by AER-3442)

The castings that survive the rulings on MANUFACTURING envelope v1 section 9.5 (`docs/engine/CASTINGS_H.md`): three part numbers, four castings per engine. Drawn by PROPULSION. The models are for a casting and first-op RFQ. They are not production drawings. Nothing here has been made, measured or run, and this is an experimental engine design, not a certified or certifiable one.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `H-101.step` | crankcase half LH, cylinder bank merged in, A357-T6 |
| `H-102.step` | crankcase half RH, cylinder bank merged in, A357-T6 |
| `H-201.step` | cylinder head, A356-T6, one part number on both banks (the RH bank takes it rotated 180 degrees about the vertical axis) |
| `H-000_casting_set.step` | the four castings assembled, for a fit check; not a part to quote |
| `H-301.step` | crankshaft, one-off billet, Rev A for quotation (AER-3439; `docs/engine/CRANK_H301.md`). Built by `h_crank.py` from `h301_params.json` |
| `H-301_drawing.png` | the H-301 quoting print: side and end views, shaded view, notes. Drawn by `render_h301.py`; `h301.json` holds its mass, box and feature list |
| `h_stack.json` | the rod / piston / liner / deck stack the castings are drawn to. Written by `analysis/engine_crank/stack_h.py` (AER-3442); read by `h_castings.py` together with `h301_params.json`, so the castings take H-301's pitch and pin offsets from the crank's own file |
| `crank_in_case.py`, `crank_in_case.json` | H-301 turned through 360 degrees in 15-degree steps inside H-101 and H-102, with the overlap volume at each step |
| `PART_LIST.md` | part numbers, alloys, quantities, model boxes and masses, and every datum and critical bore with its location. Generated by `h_castings.py`; do not edit by hand |
| `h_castings.json` | the same numbers, machine-readable, plus the overlap check between mating parts |
| `*_views.png`, `H-000_casting_set.png` | shaded renders of the STL exports, for review only |

## What "coarse" means here

- **Drawn:** the outer envelope; the crank bay and the main bulkheads; every datum (machined datums A/B/C and the as-cast 3-2-1 locating pads for the first setup); every critical bore: main housings, cam bore, lifter bores, liner registers, head studs, main studs, the main oil gallery, valve seat-insert and guide bores, both plug sets, injector bosses, ports, coolant bosses.
- **Not drawn:** the case perimeter flange and its roughly 30 bolts, ribs, oil cross-drillings, fillets, draft, the combustion-chamber shape (a flat 8 mm placeholder), the port shapes (straight cylinders), and the coolant transfer passages between the case jacket and the head (they depend on the Darton MID flange, [Q]).
- **Shape is near-net as-machined.** The foundry adds stock on every machined face to each part's ISO 8062 callout in `PART_LIST.md` (CT11 / RMA G: about 7 mm on the split face and the deck until the foundry states its grade). The earlier 1.5-3 mm (envelope 1.2/1.3) is a long-run, machine-moulded figure, and MANUFACTURING withdrew it on AER-3442.
- **The masses are model masses at 2.68 g/cm^3.** The case halves have solid bulkheads and 8 mm walls with no ribs or lightening, so 20.9 kg is an upper bound. The set (2 x 20.9 + 2 x 7.1 = 56 kg) sits inside MANUFACTURING's six-casting estimate of 50-64 kg (envelope 9 envelopes summed).

## Frame

Millimetres. x along the crank axis from the case front face (propeller end) toward the rear; y up; z outboard, +z the LH bank. The case split plane is z = 0 and the decks are at z = +/-246 (+/-235 before AER-3442). The head's own frame (in `PART_LIST.md`) is x along the row from its centre, y up, z from the deck outward.

## Regenerate

From a MAOS-ICE checkout, on a machine with the `maos` tools:

```sh
mkdir -p /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3331_h
cp cad/concept_h/{h_castings.py,check_step.py,crank_in_case.py,h301_params.json,h_stack.json,H-301.step} /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3331_h/

# build the three parts, export STEP + STL, write PART_LIST.md and h_castings.json (about 30 s)
maos freecad -c "OUT='/workspace/cad/aer3331_h'; exec(open(OUT + '/h_castings.py').read())"

# read each STEP back and confirm it is one valid solid
maos freecad -c "OUT='/workspace/cad/aer3331_h'; exec(open(OUT + '/check_step.py').read())"

# turn H-301 through a revolution inside the case halves; writes crank_in_case.json (about 15 s)
maos freecad -c "OUT='/workspace/cad/aer3331_h'; exec(open(OUT + '/crank_in_case.py').read())"

# renders (host Python with numpy and matplotlib; about 20 s)
python3 cad/concept_h/render_h.py /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3331_h

cp /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3331_h/{H-101,H-102,H-201,H-000_casting_set}.step \
   /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3331_h/{PART_LIST.md,h_castings.json,crank_in_case.json} cad/concept_h/
```

FreeCAD 0.21.2 (OCCT 7.6), as installed in the maos-tools container on 2026-10-16. The STL files are not committed (about 140 MB); they are regenerated by the first command.

Checks run at the commit that added these files: each STEP reads back as exactly one solid and `isValid()` is true; the overlap between case and case, and between each head and its deck, is 0 mm^3.

Checks at the AER-3442 revision: the same three, plus H-301 turned through 360 degrees inside H-101 and H-102 at 15-degree steps, with **0 mm^3 of overlap at every step**. The same check against the AER-3331 castings (111.8 mm pitch) finds up to **189,000 mm^3** of overlap, so it does detect a crank that does not fit. That is a static check of the crank against the case only. Rods, pistons and liners are checked by `analysis/engine_crank/stack_h.py`.

## H-301 crankshaft (AER-3439)

H-301 is at the 122 mm bank pitch. Since AER-3442 the castings above are at the same pitch and pin offsets, read from `h301_params.json`, and at the 246 mm deck that `h_stack.json` sets.

```sh
mkdir -p /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3439_h301
cp cad/concept_h/h_crank.py cad/concept_h/h301_params.json /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3439_h301/
maos freecad -c "OUT='/workspace/cad/aer3439_h301'; exec(open(OUT + '/h_crank.py').read())"   # about 10 s
cp /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3439_h301/{H-301.step,h301.json} cad/concept_h/
python3 cad/concept_h/render_h301.py /maos-work/shared/cad/aer3439_h301
```

Checks at the commit that added it: one solid, `isValid()` true (asserted in `h_crank.py`); STEP product name carries the part number and material; model mass 24.6 kg; mesh centre of mass on the crank axis within 0.002 mm, so the counterweights face away from their pins.
